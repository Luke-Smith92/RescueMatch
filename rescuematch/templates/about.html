About RescueMatch

RescueMatch is a web application designed to work like a Tinder-style matching platform for animal rehoming across the UK.

Users can browse rescue animals, view their details, and use a limited number of free matches before needing to pay to continue seeing more animals.

Rescue centre details are hidden until a match has been made. This is because showing the location too early could discourage users from considering an animal they may otherwise feel strongly connected to.

The aim of RescueMatch is to encourage users to focus first on the animal’s personality, needs, and suitability before making a decision based on location.
